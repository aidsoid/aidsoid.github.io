Support

Happy to help. The fastest way to reach us is by email.

Email us

We usually reply within a couple of days. When reporting an issue, please mention your macOS version and what you were doing.

How do I translate text?

Select text and press your hotkey (default ⌘⌥T) or use the menu-bar item. The translation appears in a small floating window.

How do I translate something I can’t select — an image, a game, a video?

Use Translate Screen Area from the menu bar (or its hotkey) and draw a box around the text. Awnsy reads it on-device and translates it.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. The built-in on-device model works right away, offline. For top quality you can optionally add your own OpenAI or Anthropic key in Settings → Models.

Where is my API key stored?

Locally on your Mac, in the macOS Keychain. It’s never sent to us.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new Mac?

Open Settings → Awnsy Pro and choose Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings (Apple ID → Subscriptions).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

What does Awnsy require?

A Mac running macOS 15 or later. No account, no sign-up.
